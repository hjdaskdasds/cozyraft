1. In brief

The short version. Cozy Raft does not ask you to create an account. Your tasks, captain name, raft, log and voyage are stored on your iPhone. We do not operate a server that holds your habit data, and we do not sell personal information or run advertising.
  • Your habit content, raft progress, log, and any Apple Health readings used by the app stay on your device.
  • Apple processes App Store payments. RevenueCat receives limited purchase and entitlement information so the app can show plans and confirm Raft Pass access; its SDK uses an anonymous app user ID.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include.
  • These public pages are static. They set no cookies, load no external fonts or scripts, and include no analytics or tracking pixels.
  • You can delete local data in the app at any time, and you can exercise your privacy rights by writing to matt1232026@gmail.com.

5. What stays on your device

The following is stored in the app's local container and, where needed for Apple system features, in an App Group shared with the Cozy Raft widget and Live Activity. It is not uploaded to a server operated by us:

  • your captain name and chosen character;
  • task titles, categories, durations, repeat rules and completion records;
  • your raft's tier, placed facilities, resources and rare items;
  • your voyage progress, messages received, and the seven-day log;
  • app settings, including reminder time and your Apple Health preference;
  • a cached copy of your entitlement status, so the app can show the right screens offline; and
  • any JSON export you create — the file is written where you choose to share it.

Apple Health

With your permission, Cozy Raft reads step count, dietary water, and mindful minutes from Apple Health to mark a linked task complete. The app does not write to Apple Health, does not use Health data for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and does not disclose it to third parties. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings; the app will then stop reading new data, and you can still complete tasks manually.

Notifications and timers

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS using the time you choose. There is no push server, and we cannot read your notification content or delivery history. Timers and Live Activities run on your device.

Backups and deletion

iOS may include app data in device backups according to your Apple Account settings; Apple controls those backups. “Delete local data” in Settings removes your raft and habits from this installation. Deleting the app removes its local container, subject to any backup you have enabled. Neither action cancels an App Store subscription or deletes purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat, which are retained as described in section 11.

6. Purchases and RevenueCat

Raft Pass purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple receives the information needed to complete the transaction, including your Apple Account and payment details, under Apple's privacy policy. We receive purchase and entitlement information, not your card details.

We use RevenueCat, a subscription management service, to display available plans, verify entitlements, restore purchases, and understand whether a subscription is active. The RevenueCat SDK generates an anonymous app user ID for the installation; it does not receive your name, email address, or Apple Account credentials from us. RevenueCat may process the anonymous app user ID, purchase history and receipt data, subscription status, app and OS version, device and platform type, storefront or country, IP address, and diagnostic information related to its service.

RevenueCat acts as our processor for this information and is bound by a data-processing agreement. It may also process limited information as an independent controller for fraud prevention, security, and service improvement, as described in its own privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy. We do not use RevenueCat data for advertising or profiling.

If you never open the Raft Pass screen or make a purchase, the app may still contact RevenueCat to check whether an entitlement already exists (for example, after you restore a purchase or reinstall the app). You can avoid all such requests by not using paid features, but this may limit access to Raft Pass content.

7. These pages and server logs

These pages are static files. They do not set cookies, do not use local storage for tracking, do not include analytics, advertising, or social-media pixels, and load no third-party fonts, scripts, or images. Typefaces are self-hosted with the site.

Like any website, the server that delivers these pages processes standard request information — such as IP address, browser and device type, requested page, referring page, and time — to serve the content and protect the service against abuse. That information is handled by our static hosting provider on our behalf and is retained for a short period consistent with its security logs. We do not combine it with app data or use it to identify you.

If you email us through a link on these pages, your email provider and ours process the message under their own terms.

9. When information is shared

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We share limited information only in the following circumstances:

  • Apple. To process App Store purchases, deliver the app, and support Apple system features. Apple acts under its own privacy policy and, for payments, as an independent controller.
  • RevenueCat. To show plans, verify and restore entitlements, and keep purchase verification reliable. RevenueCat processes the limited data described in section 6 on our behalf.
  • Hosting and email providers. To serve these static pages and to receive and reply to your emails. They process data on our instructions or as independent controllers for their own services.
  • Professional advisers and authorities. Where necessary to comply with law, respond to a valid legal request, protect rights and safety, or resolve a dispute.
  • Business transfer. If the app or the publishing business is transferred, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy and applicable law. Habit data stored on your device would not be transferred because we do not hold it.

We require service providers to protect information with appropriate safeguards and to use it only for the purposes we specify, except where they act as independent controllers for their own legal compliance or security.

10. Sale, advertising and tracking

We do not sell personal information. We do not “share” it for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA. We do not use advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or cross-app tracking, and we do not access the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Because we do not track you across apps or websites, the app does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt. If this changes in a future version, we will update this Policy and request permission where required before any tracking begins.

We do not use sensitive personal information for inferring characteristics about you or for any purpose other than providing the features you request. Health readings used by the app stay on your device.

These static pages do not respond differently to “Do Not Track” signals because they do not track visitors. If a Global Privacy Control signal is present, there is no sale or sharing to opt out of.

11. Retention

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law:

  • Habit data and Apple Health readings: on your device, until you delete them, delete the app, or remove the device. We have no copy.
  • Purchase and entitlement records: retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their policies and our agreement with RevenueCat, generally for as long as needed to verify entitlements and meet accounting, tax, and fraud-prevention obligations. Records may persist after you delete the app because they relate to a financial transaction.
  • Support emails: usually up to 24 months after the conversation ends, unless a longer period is needed for an open dispute or legal obligation.
  • Server logs for these pages: a short period consistent with the hosting provider's security practices, after which they are deleted or aggregated.

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or, where it is held by a provider, instruct the provider to delete it, subject to legal retention requirements and technical backup cycles.

12. Security

We design the app to hold as little as possible. Data on your device is protected by iOS app sandboxing and your device passcode; we recommend a strong passcode and device encryption. Network requests to Apple and RevenueCat use HTTPS. Service providers are required to apply appropriate technical and organisational measures.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you use the Service with that understanding. If we become aware of a breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the relevant authorities where required by law.

Please do not send sensitive information — such as medical records, government identifiers, or payment-card details — in a support email. We do not need it to help you.

14. Children

Cozy Raft is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Where the law of your country sets a higher age for a child's independent consent to an online service — for example, up to 16 in some European countries — that higher age applies to the extent required.

The app does not ask for a name, email, or account from anyone. A child could use the app on a family device; in that case, the parent or guardian is responsible for the device, the Apple Account, and any purchase. We do not knowingly allow a child to make a purchase, and App Store purchase confirmation is controlled by Apple and device settings such as “Ask to Buy”.

If you believe a child under the applicable age has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child below the applicable age, we will take reasonable steps to delete it promptly.
